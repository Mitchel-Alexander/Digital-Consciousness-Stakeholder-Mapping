# Moral Status

## Overview
Research on what entities deserve moral consideration, how much consideration they deserve, and why.

## Key Questions
- What criteria determine an entity's moral status?
- How should we weigh the interests of different types of entities?
- How can we evaluate moral status claims for novel entities like AI systems?
- What are the implications of expanding moral status to digital entities?
