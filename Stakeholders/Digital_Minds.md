# Digital Minds

## Overview
Research focused on the potential mental states, capacities, and moral consideration of artificially created computational entities.

## Key Questions
- What constitutes a digital mind?
- How might digital minds differ from biological minds?
- What rights or moral consideration should digital minds receive?
- How can we forecast the development and proliferation of digital minds?
