# Philosophy of Mind

## Overview
The branch of philosophy concerned with the nature of mind, consciousness, and the relationship between mental and physical phenomena.

## Key Questions
- What is the nature of consciousness?
- How does the mind relate to the physical world?
- Can minds exist in non-biological substrates?
- What constitutes personal identity and selfhood?
