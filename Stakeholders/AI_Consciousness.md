# AI Consciousness

## Overview
Research on whether artificial systems can or will possess subjective experiences similar to human consciousness.

## Key Questions
- What are the necessary and sufficient conditions for consciousness?
- Can computational systems meet these conditions?
- How might we detect or measure consciousness in AI systems?
- What are the ethical implications if AI systems become conscious?
